SUKUNA....THE GREAT





Lord Sukuna is a well known Anime character from famous the famous Anime 'Jujutsu Kaisen'.(you can watch this anime on Jujutsu Kaisen )


Some facts about Sukuna are:

  • His full name is Ryomen Sukuna (両面宿儺). He is known as the King of Curses.

  • He originally lived over 1,000 years ago. Sukuna was a legendary jujutsu sorcerer during the Heian era, rather than originally being a normal cursed spirit.

  • He was unbelievably powerful even before becoming a curse. Other sorcerers considered him such a massive threat that they eventually had to fight him collectively.

  • The "two-faced" imagery has a real historical connection. The name Ryomen Sukuna comes from an actual figure mentioned in the ancient Japanese historical text Nihon Shoki. The fictional character takes inspiration from this figure.

  • His fingers became cursed objects. ☠️ After his death, his immense cursed energy remained in 20 indestructible fingers. These became the famous Sukuna fingers.

  • Yuji Itadori became his vessel. Yuji was able to consume one of Sukuna's fingers without immediately dying, which is extremely unusual.

  • Sukuna has his own innate domain expansion: Malevolent Shrine (Fukuma Mizushi). Unlike most Domain Expansions, it doesn't create a conventional enclosed barrier.
